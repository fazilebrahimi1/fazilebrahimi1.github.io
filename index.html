Apr – Jun 2026·MSc Capstone · Client: Rebel Solutions Group·Grade A

Uncovering and predicting revenue loss in public transport

Built a payment-failure prediction system for a European public transport operator.

  • Data pipeline: turned raw transaction logs into 42 features, with strict time-based controls so no future information leaked into training.
  • Lightweight logistic regression for real-time checks at the gate, flagging high-risk accounts without slowing passengers down.
  • Nightly XGBoost model (AUC 0.797) to prioritise back-office collection and denial-list queues.
  • Dynamic threshold matrix balancing passenger friction against financial risk, recovering 27.6% of baseline uncollected revenue.
  • Delivered a technical report, business and academic presentations, and a reproducible notebook.
MSc · Time Series Forecasting

Rossmann store sales forecasting

Forecast weekly sales for a Rossmann drugstore using real retail data (Kaggle, 2013–2015), comparing SARIMA, ARIMA, and XGBoost with lag and promotion features.

  • XGBoost was the most accurate model, with an 8.4% error (MAPE), against 12.0% for ARIMA and 18.2% for SARIMA.
  • Promotions added about €6,800 in weekly revenue, a 28% uplift.

MSc · Causal Analysis·Term project

Does higher internet penetration cause lower unemployment?

Tested whether expanding internet access reduces unemployment across countries, using World Bank panel data and regressions with country and year fixed effects. The average effect was not statistically significant, but splitting by income level showed the effect is about four times stronger in high-income countries, suggesting the benefits of internet access depend on a country's institutions.

2026·MSc · Causal Analysis

CO₂ emissions and economic activity: a panel data analysis

Estimated how per-capita CO₂ responds to per-capita GDP across 50 countries over 31 years, using six specifications: cross-section OLS, first differences with lags, and two-way fixed effects. Within-country estimates converge on an elasticity of about 0.74, robust to a trade-openness confounder check.

Built on a teaching dataset that mirrors the World Bank WDI structure.
